// about me

The person
behind the code

Hi, I'm Nicolas Graham — a recent graduate of the University of South Florida with a BS in Applied Science (Information Architecture), and I'm heading into a Master's in Cybersecurity at USF's Bellini College in Fall 2026. My work sits at the intersection of technology and humanity.

My Panamanian heritage taught me the power of community — that we rise together, not alone. Now I build technology that brings people together and creates safe spaces for those who feel unseen. 🇵🇦💙

I've built a mental health support tool, a wellness tracker designed around honesty instead of toxic positivity, a digital safety hub to protect the most vulnerable people online, and interactive experiences made to remind people they matter. The thread running through all of it: technology should care for people.

Outside of code, I moderate a Discord community of 500+ members, shoot photography drawn to nature, light, and quiet skies, and compose ambient music that holds real emotion. I connect deeply across cultures and distances — and I'm learning to point some of that same care back toward myself.

Pa'lante siempre — Always forward. 🐂💚💛
